About Rapid+ Blog

Plain-language guides on browser privacy, security, speed and how the web works, from the team that builds Rapid+ Browser.

What this blog is

Rapid+ Blog is where we explain the things we run into while building a browser: how proxies and VPNs differ, what websites can learn about you, why a page loads slowly, and what to do when a big security story breaks. We write long guides, in plain language, and link the sources we used.

Who writes it

Every article is written by the Rapid+ Team at HM Dev Studio, the independent studio that makes Rapid+ Browser for Android and Windows. When an article mentions our own browser, it says so.

How we write

  • We check claims against original sources where we can, and list them at the end of each article.
  • Breaking news is labelled as a developing story and updated when the facts change.
  • We explain what a tool does and what it does not do, including the limits of our own.

Corrections

If you find a mistake, please tell us. Email admin@rapidplusbrowser.online with the article and the sentence in question and we will look into it and fix it.

Join in

You can leave a comment under any article, vote on articles and comments, and get new articles by email. You can also find us on Facebook.

About Rapid+ Browser

Rapid+ Browser is a fast, private browser for Android and Windows with separate profiles, proxy and VPN mode, ad blocking and a built-in password manager. It is free. Learn more or download it.
