Android 17 Security and Privacy Features: Scam Call Checks, One-Time Location and More

A plain guide to the new Android scam, theft and location protections announced by Google, and what to switch on today.

Rapid+ Team · 24 Nov 2026 · 5 min read

On this page

Written on 24 November 2026. The features below come from Google's Android security and privacy announcement of 12 May 2026, with Android Authority's report of the same day as a cross-check. Rollout phrases such as "in the coming weeks" date from May, and we did not check which features have reached phones since. Availability also depends on your phone maker and country.

Android 17 brings a long list of security changes, with new protection against scam calls, a way to share your location just once, limits on what a thief can do with a stolen phone, and a check on APK files before Chrome downloads them. This guide lists what Google announced, in plain words, and what each feature means for you. It also says which ones you have to switch on yourself.

The short version

  • Scam calls: "Verified financial calls" flags spoofed calls pretending to be your bank. It starts with Revolut, Itaú and Nubank on Android 11 and newer.
  • Location: a new button shares your precise location only temporarily, and an indicator appears when an app reads your location.
  • Chrome: if Safe Browsing is on, Chrome checks an APK for known malware before you download it.
  • Theft protection: "Mark as lost" can lock a phone with biometrics, and PIN guessing is limited more strictly.
  • One-time codes: Android hides sensitive codes from most apps for three hours.

Scam and call protection

Verified financial calls

Google describes a new phone call spoofing protection feature, built to keep your money and personal information safe. It starts rolling out on Android 11 and newer with Revolut, Itaú and Nubank, with more banks to follow later in the year. The idea is that your phone can tell when a call that claims to be from your bank really comes from it.

Live Threat Detection

Live Threat Detection gets new warnings about suspicious behaviour, including SMS forwarding and accessibility overlay abuse. Google gave no version or date for these.

Scam detection for chat notifications

Google lists scam detection for chat notifications as part of Advanced Protection on Android 17.

Chrome on Android checks APKs

If Safe Browsing is turned on and you try to download an app file from a website, Google says it will evaluate the APK for known malware and stop you before you download it. Google gave no version or date. Android Authority says the feature had been spotted in Chrome Canary. This is the most useful change for people who install apps from outside the Play Store. Our article on Android developer verification explains the other half of that story.

Advanced Protection

Advanced Protection is a stricter mode for people at higher risk, and it gains several things on Android 17:

  • Apps that are not labelled as accessibility tools lose access to the accessibility service, which scammers abuse to watch your screen. Android Authority says this was already in Android 17 Beta 2.
  • Device-to-device sign-in and Chrome WebGPU support are turned off.
  • Scam detection for chat notifications is added.
  • USB protection is available on all Pixel phones running Android 16 or newer, and is coming to more devices.
  • Intrusion Logging is rolling out to devices on the Android 16 December update and newer. Android Enterprise support for Advanced Protection comes later in the year.

If your phone is stolen

  • Mark as lost (in Find Hub) can lock a phone with biometric authentication, disable new Wi-Fi and Bluetooth connections and hide Quick Settings.
  • Default protections: theft features turn on by default on new Android 17 devices, and extend to devices running Android 10 or higher in Argentina, Chile, Colombia, Mexico, Peru and the UK.
  • PIN guesses: Google says it has significantly reduced the number of tries before a lock-out, with longer waits after failures.
  • IMEI on the lock screen: on Android 12 or higher, a device's IMEI can be read from the lock screen so that police, makers or carriers can verify ownership. It can be turned off in settings.

Privacy and location

  • Temporary precise location: a new button lets you share your exact location for just that moment, rather than giving the app permanent access.
  • Location indicator: a marker at the top of the screen shows when your location is being read, and tapping it lists recent apps.
  • Contact picker: apps can ask for specific contacts only, with temporary access and limited fields, in place of your whole address book.
  • OTP hiding: Android hides sensitive security codes from most apps for three hours. Android Authority ties this to Android 17.

Under the hood

  • Android 17 introduces AISeal with pKVM, which isolates on-device data used by AI features.
  • Android OS verification arrives on Pixel phones first, with a public record of Google app builds.
  • The disable 2G toggle lets carriers turn 2G off by default.
  • Post-quantum cryptography is introduced.

What to switch on today

  1. Turn on Safe Browsing in Chrome so that APK checks and warnings can work.
  2. Use the one-time location option for apps that do not need constant access, and review which apps have precise location.
  3. Check Find Hub and make sure it is on for your phone.
  4. Set a longer PIN than four digits, and add biometrics.
  5. Consider Advanced Protection if you are a journalist, activist or otherwise at higher risk.
  6. Install updates as soon as your phone offers them. New features arrive only with them.

The same approach works on any phone: our shopping safety guide and passkey guide cover habits that protect you whichever system you use.

Frequently asked questions

Is Android 17 out?

Google announced the features on 12 May 2026, and some were already in Android 17 beta builds. Whether your phone has received the final release depends on your maker, so check Settings, System, System update.

Which phones get these features?

It varies. Some, like Verified financial calls, target Android 11 and newer. Others are Android 17 only, and some start on Pixel phones.

Does Chrome now block malware apps?

If Safe Browsing is on, Chrome will check an APK for known malware before the download finishes and stop it if it is flagged. It checks only for known malware, so it is not a guarantee.

How do I share my location just once?

Android 17 adds a location button that apps can use to ask for your precise location temporarily. If an app uses it, you choose to share only for that moment.

Can apps still read my one-time codes?

Android 17 hides sensitive security codes from most apps for three hours. Your messaging app and some system parts still see them.
