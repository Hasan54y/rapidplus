Highlights

Control over how you browse

The things other mobile browsers leave out.

Multiple profiles

Keep work, personal and shopping separate. Each profile has its own tabs, history, bookmarks, cookies and settings. Switch instantly and your pages stay exactly where you left them.

Learn more

Proxy & VPN mode

Route your browsing through your own SOCKS5 or HTTP proxy and turn it on with one tap.

Learn more

User-agent switcher

Appear as Chrome, Firefox, Safari, iPhone 6, iPhone 13 or iPhone 17, or stay on Default. Handy for sites that behave differently per device.

Learn more

Password manager

Rapid+ offers to save your passwords when you sign in and fills them in later. They are encrypted on your device and never uploaded.

Private tabs

Private tabs leave no history or saved passwords, wipe their cookies when closed, and block screenshots.

Smart address bar

Start typing and Rapid+ suggests pages from your history and bookmarks, ranked by relevance, how often you visit and how recently.
